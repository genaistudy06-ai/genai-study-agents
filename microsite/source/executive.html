Executive brief · Open-source dependency risk

Fix what matters first, and stop new risk before it ships.

Everlit is our in-house platform for vulnerabilities in the open-source packages our code depends on. It ranks every finding by real business risk, stops risky changes at the pull request, gets fixes made and verified, and gives leadership one trustworthy view of whether we are getting safer.

02 How it works

One decision layer between our code, our tools and our people.

Everlit doesn't replace the scanners we already pay for. It brings their results together, adds our business context, decides what to do, and makes sure it gets done.

Scope. Everlit starts with open-source dependencies across 8 ecosystems (npm, PyPI, Maven, Go, RubyGems, NuGet, Cargo, Composer), where a fix is usually an upgrade that can be tested and proven by a rescan. Containers, code and secrets come next, with built-in open-source scanners (Trivy for containers, Semgrep for code, Gitleaks for secrets) or the scanners we already license, so no vendor connector is required. Container findings use the same score, because they carry CVEs; code and secret findings have no CVE, so they get their own severity rules alongside our exposure and criticality.

Continuous loop: every fix is confirmed by a rescan, and results feed the dashboards and future prioritization.

03 Stop risk at the source

The cheapest vulnerability to fix is the one that never merges.

Everlit works at two points in the delivery pipeline: it stops new risk at the pull request, and it finds and fixes existing risk already in our code.

Prevent: new risk stopped hereOnly what the change introduces can block, so teams are never held up by old debt. The AI review flags risky code patterns with a suggested fix while the author is still working on it.
Cure: existing risk found and fixed hereScans on every push and on a schedule. Findings are ranked by real risk, fixed by tested PRs or agents, and closed only when a rescan proves it.
04 Why build this, not buy another tool

The tools we have find problems. None of them closes the loop with our context.

Each kind of tool on the market does one part well. Everlit is the layer that joins them: it reuses the scanners we already license and adds the decisions and follow-through they leave out.

Grades are for each category as a whole; the named products are examples, and capabilities vary by product, tier and configuration. Everlit works alongside the scanners we already own (bring your own) rather than replacing them. Commercial risk-aggregation platforms (for example ArmorCode, Apiiro, Nucleus Security or Brinqa) are the closest alternative, and buying one remains the fallback if the pilot misses its targets.

Scanners find. Everlit proves.

Every scan carries a coverage receipt, every fix is verified by a rescan, and every decision records its policy version in an audit log nobody can quietly edit. That evidence is what auditors and leadership ask for.

The cost moves from licences to people

Exposure, criticality and ownership live with us, and existing scanner contracts plug in through adapters. In return we carry the build, the hosting and a standing platform team.

A clear exit if it doesn't deliver

The proposal is to start in advisory mode with one team and measure against our own baseline. If the targets aren't met, we fall back to buying a platform with the requirements already written.

08 Rollout and investment

Start small, prove the signal, then enforce and scale.

Only Phase 1 is asked for now. Each later phase is costed and approved at the previous exit, so the scope can't grow faster than the team that runs it.

Phase 1 · 8–12 week build, 4–6 week pilot

Build the core, pilot with one team

  • Open-source dependencies, on one Git provider and one CI platform
  • Onboard the pilot team's repos and one scanner source
  • Priority scoring, policy and fix deadlines
  • Advisory mode only: nothing is blocked yet
  • Capture baselines in weeks 1–2
Exit check: signal quality proven; targets ratified
Phase 2 · after pilot exit

Enforce and expand

  • Turn on merge blocking once false positives are under 5%
  • CI-tested automatic fix PRs
  • Jira tickets, executive reporting, access control
  • Hand over to the named platform team within 4 weeks
Exit check: more teams onboarded; fix time trending down
Phase 3 · scale

Prevent more, automate more

  • AI review in the PR gate (advisory, self-hosted by default)
  • SDLC agents for complex fixes, with human approval
  • Internal package advisories for private code
  • Broader scanner and CI coverage
Exit check: auto-fix and backlog targets met; effort sized at pilot exit
